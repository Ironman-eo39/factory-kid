# DIFFERENCE LOG — Plonko (content/characters/plonko/)

Original character created 2026-10-15 for the kid-bait physics channel.
Inspired by the *generic archetype* of 2025 "brainrot" meme characters
(anthropomorphic wooden log carrying a bat). This log documents deliberate
differences from that archetype. No names, marks, text, logos, or designs
are copied from any existing character.

## Deliberate differences

1. **Name**: "Plonko" — invented for this project, shares nothing with any
   existing character name.
2. **Body**: rounded log with darker cut-wood end rings. Archetype: plain
   featureless log.
3. **Bands**: three teal decorative bands around the body — archetype has none.
4. **Limbs**: stubby angled arms and round feet — archetype has no limbs.
5. **Weapon**: flat teal paddle-club, not a plain wooden bat.
6. **Face**: channel house-style happy-face decal (procedural, same style as
   all other channel characters) — not a copied expression.
7. **Palette**: warm oak + teal accents — distinct from the archetype's look.
8. **Motion**: idle bob + wobble tween; squash-and-recover on impact.

## Estimate

Geometry, name, palette, accessories, and motion differ across the majority
of the design — comfortably above the 20–30% project floor. No text, marks,
or logos appear anywhere on the character.
